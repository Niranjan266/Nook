Installing it

  1. Tap Download above.
  2. Open the file when it finishes — check your notification shade or Downloads.
  3. Android will say it does not usually allow installs from this source. Tap Settings, allow it for your browser, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open, and sign in as usual.

That warning is normal for any app not installed from the Play Store — it is Android telling you where the file came from, not that anything is wrong with it. The app is signed by us and only ever talks to nook.niranjand.in.
